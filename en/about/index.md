This blog records technical notes from my work and study, mainly in the field of embedded software, and will continue to broaden its scope. Learning never ends, so let life bloom.

I am an embedded software engineer. I graduated from university in Nanjing in 2016 and moved to Hangzhou to begin my career. I also met the best girl in the world there—thank you, my dear Piggy, for walking forward together.

Technically, I focus on Bluetooth, Zigbee, and Wi-Fi network protocols, FreeRTOS and Linux operating systems, driver development, and system software architecture.

In the industry, I focus on smart home (six years of experience) and am familiar with IoT cloud platforms such as Alibaba Cloud and Tuya.
